Questions

The link says the app isn't available

Joining the group can take a few minutes to reach Google Play. Wait 10 minutes and try again. Make sure Play on your phone uses the same Google account you joined the group with.

I have an iPhone

Sorry, these apps are Android only for now.

Do the apps cost anything or collect my data?

They are free, with no ads and no accounts. None of them use the internet: your videos, scores and times stay on your phone.

How do I send feedback or report a bug?

Email shootappz@gmail.com, or leave private feedback on the app's page in Google Play.

How do I stop testing?

Open the Become a tester link again and tap Leave the program. Please wait until the 14 days are over if you can.
